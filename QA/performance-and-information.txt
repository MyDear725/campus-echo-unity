2026-10-22 · Performance and information-building exterior

Performance changes
- GPU skinning enabled for Windows; original VRoid bones, expressions and textures retained.
- Remove per-renderer exterior property blocks from trees and roads. Materials that actually need the exterior-lighting flag use a shared runtime material variant, preserving batching/instancing.
- Static-batch only immutable library/cafeteria visual meshes after load, inside the entry transition. Keep text, doors, lifts, hinges, animated objects and rigidbodies separate. Keep existing interaction transforms, colliders and seat IDs.
- Share depth-tested world-lettering materials per font; do not modify shared GUI font materials.
- 2x MSAA, two exterior shadow cascades over 85 m, lower far-tree LOD bias; keep native render scale. Near buildings and furniture are retained.
- Normal player frame limit raised from 60 to 120, independent of v-sync. This is a cap, not a guaranteed frame rate.

Hardware: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU; AMD Ryzen 7 7840H.
